# Definition of Done

- Acceptance criteria are met and traceable.
- Relevant tests and checks have been run; results are recorded.
- Security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, and performance concerns are addressed where relevant.
- Documentation and operational notes are updated.
- Planning artifacts contain no unresolved placeholders (`python scripts/check_placeholders.py <paths>`); deferred values cite an issue or date.
- Review findings are resolved or explicitly accepted by the responsible human.
- Dependencies and risks are documented.
- No secrets are exposed.
- No production release occurs without explicit human approval.
