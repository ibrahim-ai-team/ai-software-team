## Checked and kept

- **Specialist set:** `database-engineer`, `ui-ux-designer`, `technical-research`, `performance-engineer`, `sre-observability-engineer`, `release-manager` and `documentation-manager` each own a distinct deliverable and are used only when needed. None were merged or removed.
- **Process:** phases, recovery, retry limits, claim checks, evidence rules and the human merge gate in the contract already matched the vision and were not rewritten.
- **Security:** deny rules, MCP merge-tool denial, read-only reviewer, branch protection on `main` (PR, 4 required checks, no bypass) are unchanged.
- **Skills:** concise but operational; only `agile-planning` contradicted the vision. Deeper skill rewrites were not needed for the pilot.
