# Turn requests into testable requirements

## When to use

Use this skill during discovery or when requirements are incomplete, ambiguous, conflicting, or changing.

## Required inputs

The idea or request, the users and their goals, known constraints, and the latest sponsor decisions that bound the scope.

## Procedure

Clarify users, goals, in-scope and out-of-scope behavior, constraints, permissions, data, edge cases, failure modes, and measurable acceptance criteria. Separate facts, assumptions, decisions, and open questions.

## Expected outputs

- Actors, goals, in/out of scope, constraints, and permissions
- Functional/non-functional requirements, edge/failure cases, and acceptance criteria
- Explicit facts, assumptions, decisions, and open questions

## Quality checks

- Ask only questions that materially change direction or safety; document reversible assumptions.
- Make acceptance criteria observable and testable.
- Trace requirements to stories/tests and identify conflicts before implementation.
