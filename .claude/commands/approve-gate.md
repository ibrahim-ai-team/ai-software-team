# Approve gate

Summarize the decision requiring sponsor input in plain language:

- Decision and why it matters
- Options and trade-offs
- Recommended option and supporting evidence
- Cost, security, privacy, access, deployment, or irreversible consequences
- What can safely continue without the decision

Pause only the gated action. Continue independent, reversible work. Never interpret silence as approval and never claim a gate was approved unless the user explicitly approves it.
