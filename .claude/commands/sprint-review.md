# Sprint review

Ask `qa-automation` for verification evidence and `project-manager` for delivery state; the coordinator reconciles source records and prepares the review. Use `test-strategy-automation` and `agile-planning` skills as relevant.

Review the completed sprint with the human sponsor and delivery team.

## Inputs

- Sprint goal and planned backlog
- Completed and incomplete stories
- Pull requests, CI results, test evidence, and release artifacts
- Acceptance feedback and unresolved risks

## Procedure

1. Compare the sprint outcome with its goal and acceptance criteria.
2. Include only completed work backed by links or observable evidence.
3. List incomplete work with its reason and recommended backlog disposition.
4. Summarize demonstrated functionality, quality results, known defects, and risks.
5. Record sponsor feedback and decisions without inventing approval.
6. Produce a concise review using templates/sprint-review-template.md.

Do not claim a feature is delivered, tested, or deployed without evidence. Do not merge pull requests.
